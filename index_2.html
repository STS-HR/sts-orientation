Welcome to STS Electronic Recycling

We are glad to have you on the team. Whether you work on the production floor, in the warehouse, on a truck, or in the office, your work helps businesses across the country dispose of their electronics securely and responsibly.

Who we are

STS Electronic Recycling is an R2v3 Certified IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) provider and electronics recycler based in Jacksonville, Texas. We handle computer, laptop, and tablet recycling, IT liquidation, logistics, and data destruction for businesses in all 50 states. Our certifications include R2v3 and NAID AAA, and we operate a zero-landfill program out of our 250,000 square foot facility.

Our mission

To provide responsible, efficient, and environmentally sound electronic recycling solutions to our customers, partners, and communities.

Why your job matters. Many of the devices that come through our doors still hold our clients' data. Customers trust STS to protect that information and to recycle every device the right way. Every person on the team, in every department, is part of keeping that promise.

Our core values

Integrity

Do the right thing, even when no one is watching. Be honest about your time, your work, and your mistakes.

Safety

Wear your PPE, speak up about hazards, and look out for the people working around you.

Teamwork

Help each other, share what you know, and treat every coworker with respect.

Environmental Stewardship

Keep materials out of landfills and handle every device the responsible way.

Your First Day: Orientation

Quick Version
  • Orientation is held every Wednesday, 9:00 to 10:00 AM, with the Office of Human Resources.
  • Finish your new hire documents in Paylocity ahead of time if you received the link.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes. You will be issued your PPE during orientation.
  • Your fingerprint must be enrolled in the time clock before you can start work.

Before orientation

  • Check your email and phone. The Office of Human Resources will send you a link to Paylocity so you can complete your new hire documents online before orientation. Finishing these ahead of time makes your orientation go faster.
  • Bring your identification documents. Federal law requires every new employee to complete Form I-9 and show original documents that prove identity and work authorization (for example, a driver's license and Social Security card, or a U.S. passport).
  • Have your bank information ready (routing and account number) if you want to set up direct deposit.
  • Bring your phone. You will use the Paylocity employee app to complete paperwork during orientation.
  • Dress for the floor. Wear closed-toe, closed-heel, non-slip shoes and clothing that follows the dress code.

What happens at orientation

  1. Arrive by 9:00 AM. Check in with the Office of Human Resources. Please be on time, since orientation starts promptly.
  2. Receive your orientation folder. You will get your new hire folder and paperwork packet.
  3. Get your PPE. You will be issued the personal protective equipment you need before you do any work.
  4. Complete your paperwork in Paylocity. HR will walk you through the app and review every entry with you.
  5. Review and sign. You will read, sign, and accept your orientation documents and acknowledgments.
  6. Ask questions. We finish with an open discussion. No question is too small.

After orientation

Your supervisor will meet you, introduce you to your team, and enroll your fingerprint in the time clock system. You may not begin work until your fingerprint has been successfully registered.

Paylocity: Employee Self-Service

Quick Version
  • Paylocity is where you see your pay, hours, tax forms, and benefits.
  • Download the Paylocity app (iPhone or Android) or log in at app.paylocity.com.
  • It is your responsibility to check it regularly and keep your information current.

What you can do in Paylocity

Pay

  • View and download pay stubs and earnings history
  • Manage direct deposit and payment elections
  • Access W-2s and year-end tax documents

Time, benefits and profile

  • Review your timesheet and verify hours worked
  • View and manage benefits enrollment
  • Update your address, phone, and emergency contacts
  • Submit time off requests

Getting started

  1. Watch for your invitation. The Office of Human Resources will send you a link and login information during onboarding.
  2. Create your account. Set your password and security questions, then verify your identity when prompted on first login.
  3. Download the app. Search "Paylocity" in the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same credentials.
  4. Check your profile. Make sure your address, phone number, emergency contact, and direct deposit are correct.

Your responsibility: log in regularly to review your pay stubs, confirm your hours, and keep your personal information up to date. If something looks wrong, tell your supervisor or the Office of Human Resources right away. Mistakes are much easier to fix when they are caught early.

Locked out or having trouble logging in? Contact the Office of Human Resources or your supervisor.

Time Clock and Getting Paid

Quick Version
  • Clock in before you start working and clock out as soon as your shift ends.
  • Clock out and back in for your unpaid lunch.
  • Never clock in or out for someone else. It is time fraud and grounds for immediate termination.
  • Clock not reading your finger? Tell your supervisor before you start work.

All STS employees clock in and out using the company's biometric fingerprint time clock. Accurate timekeeping is how you get paid correctly and how STS stays in compliance with wage and hour laws.

SystemBiometric fingerprint time clock
Clock inBefore beginning any work activity
Clock outImmediately at the end of your shift, and for your lunch break
ProblemsReport immediately to your direct supervisor
Timesheet reviewWeekly. Report anything that looks wrong promptly.

Setup and registration

  • Every new employee must be enrolled in the fingerprint time clock before their first shift.
  • Your supervisor will coordinate your fingerprint enrollment on your first day.
  • You will not be permitted to begin work until your fingerprint has been successfully registered.

Daily clock-in and clock-out rules

  • Always clock in before you begin work, and clock out immediately at the end of your shift. Do not work "off the clock" for any reason.
  • Do not leave the premises before clocking out.
  • If the time clock will not read your fingerprint or is not working, notify your supervisor before you start work so your time can be recorded correctly.
  • If you forget to clock in or out, report it to your supervisor the same day.
  • Timesheets are reviewed weekly. Check your hours in Paylocity and report any discrepancies promptly.

Zero tolerance: clocking in or out for another employee, or asking someone to clock for you, is time fraud and will result in immediate termination for everyone involved.

Getting paid

  • Payday is Friday. Your pay stub is available in Paylocity on payday.
  • Direct deposit is the fastest and most secure way to get paid. You can set it up or change it in Paylocity.
  • Paper checks are delivered by your department supervisor, manager, or lead, who picks them up from the Office of Human Resources. Checks are handed only to the employee named on the check.
  • You will choose your method of pay (direct deposit or paper check) during orientation.

Attendance, Call-In and No-Call/No-Show

Quick Version
  • Going to be late or absent? Call your supervisor at least 2 hours before your shift starts.
  • It must be a phone call and you must speak with a supervisor or manager. A text or voicemail alone does not count.
  • Any no-call/no-show can lead to corrective action up to termination. Three in a row is treated as a voluntary resignation.

Everyone, every day: report to work on time, ready to work, and stay for your entire scheduled shift. Your team counts on you being there, and when someone is missing without notice, everyone else has to cover.

How to call in

  1. Call at least 2 hours before your scheduled start time. Earlier is always better.
  2. Make a phone call. Text messages alone are not acceptable.
  3. Speak directly with your supervisor or a manager. A voicemail alone does not meet the requirement. If you reach voicemail, keep trying, or call another manager in your department.
  4. Give clear information: your name, department, shift time, the reason for your absence, and when you expect to return.
  5. Keep your supervisor updated if your absence will run longer than you first reported.

If you physically cannot call (for example, you are in the hospital), someone may call your supervisor on your behalf. The call must still come before your shift starts. For extraordinary circumstances, the Office of Human Resources has the final say.

Excused vs. unexcused absences

Excused absence

Requires at least 2 of these 4:

  1. A doctor's note, jury duty notice, school note, or similar proof
  2. Your supervisor approved the time in advance
  3. You have enough PTO to cover it
  4. You called in at least 2 hours before your shift

Three or more consecutive sick or injury days require a doctor's note authorizing your return before you come back.

Unexcused absence

  • Fewer than 2 of the 4 conditions are met
  • You still must notify your supervisor no later than your scheduled start time
  • Counts as 1 full disciplinary incident

How incidents are counted

EventResult
Unexcused absence1 incident
Tardy (late arrival) or early departure½ incident
2 or more unexcused absences in 30 days, or 8 or more in 12 monthsExcessive absenteeism, grounds for termination
Any single no-call/no-showSubject to corrective action, up to and including termination
No-call/no-show for 3 consecutive daysJob abandonment, treated as a voluntary resignation

Repeated unexcused absences, tardiness, or leaving early may result in corrective action even when you do call in.

Accommodations: if you need a disability or religious accommodation related to attendance, submit a written request to the Office of Human Resources. For sick leave, unpaid leave, or FMLA questions, see the Employee Handbook or contact the Office of Human Resources.

Vacation, Time Off and Holidays

Quick Version
  • Regular full-time employees earn 5 days of paid vacation after 6 months, then 5 more days each new year while in good standing.
  • Your balance can never be more than 10 days.
  • Request time off at least 2 weeks ahead (4 weeks for Thanksgiving or Christmas).
  • STS observes 6 paid holidays.

How vacation is earned

  • You earn your first 5 days of paid vacation after 6 months of employment.
  • Starting with the first new year after that, employees in good standing accrue 5 more days each year.
  • Unused vacation rolls over, but your balance can never exceed 10 days at any time.
  • You do not accrue vacation while on a personal leave of absence or while suspended.
  • If a company holiday falls during your vacation, you get one additional day of vacation.
  • Unused vacation is not paid out if you are terminated or voluntarily leave.

How to request time off (TOR)

  1. Plan ahead. Give at least 2 weeks' notice for standard vacation, and 4 weeks' notice for time off around Thanksgiving or Christmas.
  2. Submit your request through Paylocity, or turn in a Time Off Request (TOR) form directly to your manager.
  3. Get it signed. A TOR form must be completely filled out and signed by both you and your supervisor or manager.
  4. Wait for approval before making plans. When requests conflict, they are decided based on employment, seniority, and business needs.

Paid holidays

New Year's Day
Memorial Day
Independence Day
Labor Day
Thanksgiving Day
Christmas Day

Holiday pay rules

  • If a holiday falls on a weekend, it is observed on the Friday before or Monday after, as determined by management.
  • If you work an assigned holiday (confirmed by your manager), you receive double pay.
  • To be eligible for holiday pay, you must work your scheduled day before and the day after the holiday, unless your manager approves otherwise in advance.

Safety and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Quick Version
  • Safety glasses at all times in production and warehouse areas. Gloves when handling electronics or sharp items. Ear protection in loud areas.
  • STS provides your PPE at no cost. Report damaged PPE for a replacement.
  • Hurt at work, even slightly? Tell your supervisor immediately.

The safety of every STS employee is our top priority. All employees must wear the appropriate PPE while working on the production floor, in the warehouse, or in any designated PPE-required area. That includes office staff visiting the floor.

Required PPE for all employees

PPEWhen it is requiredWhy
Safety glassesAt all times in production and warehouse areasProtects against flying debris, dust, and sharp materials
GlovesWhen handling electronic components, sharp materials, or anything that could cut or contaminatePrevents cuts and contact with hazardous materials
Ear protection (ear buds)In high-noise areasPrevents hearing damage from equipment and machinery
Safety footwearClosed-toe, non-slip shoes at all times on the floor. Steel-toe or safety-rated where posted or assigned.Protects against falling objects and slips

PPE guidelines

  • PPE is provided by STS at no cost to you. You are responsible for wearing it and taking care of it.
  • Report damaged or worn PPE to your supervisor immediately for a replacement.
  • Some tasks require additional PPE, such as a hi-vis vest. Your supervisor will tell you what your job requires.
  • Refusing to wear required PPE, or removing or bypassing safety features, is a safety violation and may result in disciplinary action.

General safety expectations

  • Report unsafe conditions, spills, damaged equipment, and near misses to your supervisor right away.
  • Only operate equipment you have been trained and authorized to use.
  • Keep walkways, exits, and your work area clean and clear.
  • Know where the exits are in your area, and follow your supervisor's instructions in an emergency.

If you get hurt at work

  1. Report it to your supervisor immediately, no matter how minor it seems. Do not wait until the end of your shift.
  2. Get care. Your supervisor will arrange first aid or send you to an approved treating facility designated by the Office of Human Resources. In an emergency, call 911.
  3. Complete an incident report with your supervisor as soon as possible.
  4. Tell the provider the injury is work-related, and follow any work restrictions they give you.

Post-accident drug and alcohol testing may be required after a workplace incident. Reporting an injury never waives your right to file a workers' compensation claim if your condition gets worse later.

Dress Code

Quick Version
  • Come to work clean, well-groomed, and dressed safely for your work area.
  • Closed-toe, closed-heel, non-slip shoes at all times on the floor.
  • Not sure if something is OK? Ask before your shift.

These rules apply to managers, leads, and all warehouse, operations, logistics, and facilities employees. Office and customer-facing staff follow a business-casual standard, but the same footwear and PPE rules apply to you whenever you are on the warehouse floor.

Required

  • Closed-toe, closed-heel, non-slip shoes at all times on the floor
  • Steel-toe or safety-rated footwear where posted or assigned by your supervisor
  • All PPE for your task or area: safety glasses, gloves, hearing protection, hi-vis vest, and so on
  • Hair tied back or secured when working near moving equipment
  • Fitted clothing when working near conveyors or machinery

Not allowed

  • Sandals, open-toe shoes, high heels, or thin-sole canvas sneakers on the floor
  • Loose or baggy clothing, dangling scarves, lanyards, or unsecured jewelry near machinery
  • Removing or bypassing required PPE or safety features
  • Clothing with offensive, discriminatory, or explicit language or images
  • Provocative or revealing clothing
  • Visible undergarments

Shorts and sleeveless shirts are only permitted on the floor where your supervisor confirms no task-specific hazard applies. Closed-toe safety footwear is still required.

Enforcement: violations, including unsafe footwear or missing PPE, may result in being sent home to change and corrective action up to termination. Accommodations: religious or disability accommodations are available. Submit a written request to the Office of Human Resources, which is the only authority on questions or exceptions to this policy.

Conduct, Workplace Policies and Discipline

Quick Version
  • Be on time, be respectful, work safely, and protect company and client property and information.
  • Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated. Report concerns to your supervisor or HR.
  • Discipline usually follows four steps, but theft, violence, or serious misconduct can mean immediate termination.

Company expectations and standards of conduct

STS holds every employee to a high standard of professionalism, accountability, and respect. Every team member is expected to:

  • Arrive on time and ready to work for every scheduled shift.
  • Treat all coworkers, supervisors, and customers with dignity and respect.
  • Keep your work area clean, safe, and organized at all times.
  • Follow all company policies, procedures, and safety guidelines.
  • Report unsafe conditions, accidents, or policy violations to your supervisor immediately.
  • Protect company property, equipment, and confidential information.
  • Represent STS professionally, both on-site and in the community.

Unauthorized use of company equipment or resources is strictly prohibited. Failing to meet these expectations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

General workplace policies

Respect and professionalism

Conduct yourself professionally at all times. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

Equipment and tools

Handle company equipment with care and return it to its proper location after use.

Personal devices

Keep personal phone and device use to a minimum during work hours. Use break time for personal calls and messages.

Confidentiality

Keep company operations and client information confidential. Never access, copy, photograph, or remove client devices, data, or materials except as your job requires.

Harassment, discrimination and the open door

STS is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to a workplace free of harassment and discrimination. If you experience or witness harassment, discrimination, or any behavior that makes you uncomfortable, report it to your supervisor, any manager, or the Office of Human Resources. You do not have to report to someone involved in the problem. STS does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Drug and alcohol policy

  • Using, possessing, or being under the influence of illegal drugs or alcohol at work, or reporting to work impaired, is grounds for immediate termination.
  • STS has a zero-tolerance policy for marijuana, regardless of state law or a medical marijuana card.
  • If you take a prescription or over-the-counter medication that could impair you, notify your supervisor or HR before working.
  • Testing may occur pre-employment, on reasonable suspicion, after an accident, at random for safety-sensitive positions, and on return to duty. Refusing a test is treated as a positive result.

The full Drug and Alcohol Policy (HR2511-A01-002) controls if anything here differs from it. Ask the Office of Human Resources for a copy.

Progressive discipline

When a performance or conduct problem comes up, STS generally uses the steps below to give you a fair chance to correct it.

Step 1

Verbal Warning

A documented conversation with your supervisor.

Step 2

Written Warning

A formal written notice placed in your personnel file.

Step 3

Final Warning / Suspension

Depending on how serious the incident is.

Step 4

Termination

For serious or repeated violations.

Immediate termination: theft, workplace violence, time fraud, substance use or impairment on company premises, or other gross misconduct may result in immediate termination without prior warning. STS may skip steps depending on the situation.

Employment with STS Electronic Recycling, LLC is at will. This packet does not create a contract of employment, and the company may change its policies at any time with appropriate notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm sick. Can I just text my supervisor?

No. You must call and speak with your supervisor or a manager at least 2 hours before your shift. A text or voicemail alone does not count. See How to call in.

I called but my supervisor didn't answer. What now?

Keep trying, and call another manager in your department. You need to actually speak with a supervisor or manager before your shift starts. If you cannot reach anyone, contact the Office of Human Resources.

I forgot to clock out (or in). What do I do?

Tell your supervisor the same day so your time can be corrected. Then check your timesheet in Paylocity to confirm it was fixed.

The time clock won't read my fingerprint.

Do not start working. Tell your supervisor right away so your start time is recorded correctly.

When do I get paid, and where do I see my pay stub?

Payday is Friday. Your pay stub, earnings history, and W-2s are in Paylocity, on the app or at app.paylocity.com.

When can I sign up for health insurance?

Eligible full-time employees can enroll after completing the 90-day probationary period. The Office of Human Resources will send you enrollment information.

When do I start earning vacation?

Regular full-time employees earn 5 days of paid vacation after 6 months, then 5 more days each new year while in good standing, up to a maximum balance of 10 days.

How far ahead do I need to ask for time off?

At least 2 weeks for standard vacation, and 4 weeks for time off around Thanksgiving or Christmas. Submit it in Paylocity or on a signed TOR form.

My safety glasses or gloves are damaged. Do I have to buy new ones?

No. PPE is provided by STS at no cost. Tell your supervisor and they will replace it.

I got a small cut. Do I really need to report it?

Yes. Report every injury to your supervisor immediately, no matter how minor. See If you get hurt at work.

Can I leave the building on my lunch break?

Only with prior approval from your supervisor. Remember to clock out for lunch and back in when you return.

I moved or changed my phone number. Who do I tell?

Update it yourself in Paylocity, and let the Office of Human Resources know so your records and emergency contacts stay current.

Can I wear shorts?

Only on the floor where your supervisor confirms no task-specific hazard applies. Closed-toe safety footwear is still required. Ask before your shift if you are unsure.

I have a concern I don't feel comfortable bringing to my supervisor.

Contact the Office of Human Resources directly. STS does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

  • Doctor, jury, or school note
  • Supervisor approved in advance
  • Enough PTO to cover it
  • Called in 2+ hours before shift

Unexcused

  • Fewer than 2 of the 4
  • Counts as 1 full incident
  • You still must notify your supervisor before your shift
3 or more sick days in a row requires a doctor's note to return.

10 · Safety

If you get hurt at work

  1. Tell your supervisor immediately, no matter how minor.
  2. Get care. First aid or an approved treating facility. Emergency: call 911.
  3. Complete an incident report with your supervisor.
  4. Tell the provider the injury is work-related and follow any restrictions.
Post-accident drug and alcohol testing may be required.

Required

  • Hair secured near moving equipment

Not allowed
